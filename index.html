A Few Words About Our Restaurant

Oliva is the second restaurant from Nadia and Farhan Chowdhury, who cooked in Bologna and Palermo for a decade before coming home. The name is for the olive tree in Nadia's grandmother's courtyard — the same tree that gave us our mark.

The building on Gulshan Avenue is being rebuilt around one idea: a long open kitchen with the dining room wrapped around it. Terrazzo floors, olive-wood tables, and a marble counter for eleven guests who want to eat facing the fire. We open 14 November 2026.

Our Way

How we work

01

Bought each morning

Fish from the Chittagong boats, vegetables from Savar growers, flour milled in house.

02

Cooked over fire

One wood oven, one charcoal grill, no microwaves and no heat lamps anywhere in the kitchen.

03

Served without ceremony

Proper service, no theatre. Bread and water arrive before you ask, and we never rush a table.

Our Chefs

Three people set the card between them, and all three cook every service.
